Corporate Overview

A MULTI-DISCIPLINARY
ZAMBIAN ENTERPRISE.

Jarecha Investments Limited was founded to bridge the gap between engineering infrastructure and technological innovation, providing institutions across Zambia with single-source accountability.

BUILT ON INTEGRITY, RIGOR, AND NATIONWIDE CAPACITY.

Operating across the Copperbelt, Lusaka, and surrounding provincial hubs, Jarecha Investments Limited executes projects ranging from optical fiber backbones and industrial 3-phase electrical distribution to turnkey surveillance setups and public institutional procurement.

Our registered engineering practice with the Engineering Institution of Zambia (EIZ), combined with our registration with the Zambia Public Procurement Authority (ZPPA), enables us to serve leading mining corporations, provincial referral hospitals, and civic municipal councils with complete regulatory assurance.

01. Vision

To be Zambia’s premier integrated infrastructure and technology partner, recognized for technical excellence and uncompromising reliability.

02. Mission

Deliver turnkey technology, electrical engineering, security automation, and procurement services that optimize operational safety and efficiency.

03. Governance

Committed to full statutory compliance (PACRA, Workers Compensation, NAPSA, ZRA, EIZ, ZPPA) and transparent institutional governance.
